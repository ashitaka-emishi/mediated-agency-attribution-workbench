# Project Charter

## Mission

Build a reproducible workbench that lets researchers construct, compare,
criticize, adjudicate, and publish competing agency-attribution explanations
without collapsing spiritual, psychological, social, institutional, symbolic,
and technical levels.

## In scope

- structured sources, passages, observations, events, and actors;
- mechanisms, media, manifestations, and causal pathways;
- rival and layered attribution hypotheses;
- argument graphs with support, objections, assumptions, defeaters, and falsifiers;
- human and model reliability studies;
- interpretive tradition lenses;
- separate causal and moral/theological evaluation;
- PPW evidence-module export;
- Quarto publication generated from canonical records.

## Out of scope

- automated declarations of possession or demonic control;
- diagnosis of living persons;
- numeric probability that an entity or institution is demonic;
- silent promotion of AI-generated claims;
- replacement of historical, psychological, sociological, or theological expertise.

## Human command authority

Humans control ontology changes, codebook changes, source-rights decisions,
final adjudication, spiritual-agency approval, finding promotion, PPW export,
and publication.
