# Project Charter

## Mission

Build a reproducible biblical-theological workbench that lets researchers
construct, compare, criticize, adjudicate, and publish competing
agency-attribution explanations without collapsing spiritual, textual,
psychological, social, institutional, symbolic, and historical levels.

## In Scope

- structured biblical sources, passages, observations, events, and actors;
- mechanisms, media, manifestations, and causal pathways;
- rival and layered attribution hypotheses;
- argument graphs with support, objections, assumptions, defeaters, and falsifiers;
- human and model reliability studies;
- interpretive tradition lenses;
- separate causal, textual, and moral/theological evaluation;
- reviewed publication generated from canonical records;
- future external exports only when a separate human-approved integration issue
  defines the receiving system and boundary.

## Out Of Scope

- automated declarations of possession, demonic control, or divine judgment;
- diagnosis of living persons;
- numeric probability that an entity, institution, or event is demonic;
- silent promotion of AI-generated claims;
- replacement of biblical, theological, historical, psychological,
  sociological, or literary expertise;
- positioning MAAW as related to political-pathology work. MAAW may reuse
  similar tooling patterns, but it is not a political-pathology repository.

## Human Command Authority

Humans control ontology changes, codebook changes, source-rights decisions,
final adjudication, spiritual-agency approval, finding promotion, external
exports, and publication.
