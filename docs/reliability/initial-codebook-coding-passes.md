model coding passes over a blinded packet, compares disagreements, and records

human review, spiritual-attribution approval, or publication decision.

- Mechanism: selective reception of counsel
- Medium: royal court setting and prophetic speech

  relation to Ahab's stated preference
- Alternatives: court-prophetic system, prophetic symbolic mediation, reported
  spiritual mediation, divine judicial agency

  pattern

- Theological status: `not-applicable`

## Adjudication Outcomes

### Ahab Selective Agency

Outcome: preserve as a lower-level explanation. Ahab's human agency is
necessary for the bounded royal-decision pattern, but the pass does not settle
broader theological claims.

Rival explanations remain live: court consultation, prophetic symbolic
mediation, lying spirit mediation, and divine judicial agency.

### Court-Prophetic System

Outcome: preserve as underdetermined and qualified. The court prophets are a
collective actor in the passage, while the royal counsel system may be either
an analytical institutional agent or a medium. Further evidence is required
before the distinction should affect canonical data.

Disagreement source: category confusion and missing evidence.

### Prophetic Symbolic Mediation

Outcome: preserve as a distinct contributory explanation. The coding passes
agree that prophetic speech is not merely an incidental delivery channel. The
codebook does not require revision here.

Disagreement source: missing evidence about how much weight the small packet
can place on symbolic mediation.

### Lying Spirit Mediation

Outcome: preserve as a proposed, human-gated spiritual attribution. The text
supplies independent evidence for the narrative proposal, but reliability
coding does not approve a spiritual attribution, generalize it beyond the
passage, or override lower-level explanations.

Rival explanations remain live: Ahab's agency, court consultation, prophetic
symbolic mediation, and divine judicial agency.

### Divine Judicial Agency

Outcome: preserve as a separate theological hypothesis where used, but keep it
distinct from creaturely mediation and moral evaluation. Human theological
review remains required.

Disagreement source: ambiguous definition of when a theological agency claim
should be separated from a qualifying interpretation.

## Codebook Revision From This Pass

The only in-scope codebook revision is a wording clarification: use
`compatible` when coding `explanatoryNecessity`, and reserve
`compatible-but-unnecessary` for attribution status where that status vocabulary
is in use.

No agency criterion definition is changed by this pass.
