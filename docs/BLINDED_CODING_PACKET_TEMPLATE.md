# Blinded Coding Packet Template

## Purpose

Use this template to prepare independent human or model coding passes without
showing coders the project's preferred attributions, findings, theological
conclusions, or adjudication status. The packet is a review artifact, not a
canonical finding.

No generated or model-produced coding result may be promoted to a canonical
finding without accepted human review.

## Coding Task

Coders should apply `docs/CODEBOOK.md` independently.

1. Record non-causal observations supported by the shown evidence.
2. Identify possible agents without treating mechanisms, media,
   manifestations, moral evaluations, or interpretive labels as agents.
3. Propose agency claims only after observations are recorded.
4. Identify candidate causes, mechanisms, media, and manifestations.
5. Apply the agency-criteria rules:
   - identity
   - persistence
   - directionality
   - selectivity
   - responsiveness
   - counterfactual dependence
   - explanatory necessity
   - independent evidence
   - alternatives
   - falsifiability
6. Assess lower-level explanations before invoking less observable agents.
7. Keep spiritual-attribution proposals qualified and human-review-gated.
8. Separate empirical causal assessment from moral and theological evaluation.

## Promotion Gate

Before any coding result affects canonical findings, confirm:

- Accepted human review exists.
- Source rights and provenance are adequate.
- Observations remain non-causal.
- Spiritual attributions remain proposed unless separately approved by humans.
- Moral/theological evaluation is separated from causal assessment.
- AI-generated or imported findings remain draft until human promotion.
